{%- comment -%} Finds the posts that mention a teacher, newest first, and puts them in lehrer_treffer. include.suche: the spellings to look for, e.g. ["Fisser", "Fißer"]. A post counts if its title or text contains one of them. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign lehrer_treffer = "" | split: "" -%} {%- for p in site.posts -%} {%- for s in include.suche -%} {%- if p.title contains s or p.content contains s -%} {%- assign lehrer_treffer = lehrer_treffer | push: p -%} {%- break -%} {%- endif -%} {%- endfor -%} {%- endfor -%}
